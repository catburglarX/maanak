Public services

Four things any member of the public can do here, with no account and no fee. Each one says what it needs from you and what it gives back. The last section is the boundary: what this cannot do, and the official route for it.

01

Check what the law requires on a package

Before you report anything, it helps to know what should be printed on a pre-packaged commodity. Every declaration this system tests is listed in plain language, with the provision it is attributed to and whether that attribution has been confirmed.

Needs nothing. Reference material, open to read.

What the law requires

02

Report a packaged product

Describe what you saw and attach a photograph of the package. The report enters a queue an officer works through, ordered by what the problem is rather than when it arrived. An overcharge against the printed price is treated as more urgent than a cosmetic labelling fault.

You will be given a reference to keep. Contact details are optional, and the form explains what you give up by leaving them out.

Needs: a photograph, and a description of at least 20 characters.

Report a product

03

Track the report you made

Returns the progress of your own report and nothing else. It does not return the inspection that followed, because that record contains a third party's business information.

Your reference alone is not enough. You also need the email address or telephone number you supplied, which is compared against a stored hash. That is deliberate: a reference on its own reveals nothing about you or the report.

Needs: your reference, plus the contact detail you gave.

Check your report

04

Verify a report someone has quoted at you

If a trader, an inspector or anyone else shows you a report reference, this confirms whether it was really issued and whether its content has changed since. The check compares a hash of the frozen snapshot, so an altered report fails even if the reference is genuine.

It discloses no brand, no premises, no decision and no officer. You learn that the document is authentic and unmodified, not what it says.

Needs: the reference, and the verification code printed on the report.

What this cannot do, and where to go instead

Being straight about the boundary matters more than appearing complete. This system reads what is printed on a package and tests it against declaration requirements. Everything below is outside that, and the official routes are free.

Nutrition and food safety

Nothing here assesses nutrition, ingredients, additives, allergens or whether a food is safe or good for you. No rule in this system derives from the Food Safety and Standards regulations, and it gives no dietary advice.

Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

fssai.gov.in publishes the labelling and nutrition regulations and takes complaints about unsafe or misbranded food.

Whether the contents match the label

Net quantity cannot be confirmed from a photograph. Establishing short measure means weighing or measuring the contents on verified equipment, which is an officer's physical task. Every report states this as a limit.

Controller of Legal Metrology, in your state

The department list is published at consumeraffairs.gov.in.

Whether a product is genuine

A readable barcode shows that an identifier is printed on a package. It is not evidence that the package, or what is inside it, is authentic. Counterfeiting is a separate matter with separate powers.

National Consumer Helpline

1915, or 1800-11-4000, 8:00 to 20:00 daily except national holidays, in 17 languages.

Getting money back, or a penalty imposed

This system issues no enforcement action, no penalty and no refund. It records evidence and an officer's reasoned decision. A remedy is a separate proceeding.

Consumer commission, under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Electronic filing at edaakhil.nic.in, or start with the helpline above at the pre-litigation stage.
