What Maanak is

Maanak records inspections of packaged commodities. It reads a label with optical character recognition, keeps the image alongside what was read from it, applies a versioned rule interpretation to the values an officer has reviewed, and produces a report that can be verified later without disclosing the case.

It is a record-keeping and evidence tool. It is not a regulator, not an adjudicator and not a certifying body. It issues no enforcement action of its own and has no power to do so.

Definitions

Candidate
A machine reading of one declaration, with a state such as located, unreadable or not detected. An observation, not a conclusion.
Finding
The result of applying one rule version to reviewed values. Carries the citation, the inputs and the arithmetic.
Rule version
One immutable interpretation of one requirement, with an effective window and an approval history.
Report snapshot
A frozen copy of an inspection at the moment of issue, hashed, from which the PDF and the editable document are rendered.
Officer
A person holding an account, acting within a role and a jurisdiction.

The standing of what it produces

A finding is a recorded opinion attributed to a named officer, supported by evidence and by a stated interpretation of a rule. It is not a statutory determination. It establishes no liability and imposes no penalty.

Nothing this system produces is legal advice. Nothing it produces decides a prosecution, a fine or a compounding. A report is admissible as what it is: a record of what an officer observed, what rule they applied and what they concluded, with the evidence attached.

Three outcomes are kept apart throughout, and conflating them misrepresents the record:

  • A declaration that is absent from evidence that was fully captured.
  • A declaration that could not be read, which is not a contravention of anything.
  • A declaration that was read and does not meet the requirement applied.

Only the third is a compliance failure. The software will not report the second as one, and no configuration makes it do so.

Accuracy of rule interpretations

The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 came into force on 1 April 2011 under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009. The eleven rule interpretations that ship with this software are drawn from that framework.

None has been verified against a gazette notification. Each arrives with its legal authority flag set to false and a note recording that its citation, its quoted requirement and its effective date all need confirmation by somebody qualified to give it. That flag appears on screen and prints on every report.

Several values are explicit placeholders. The minimum character height is set to a single figure when the prescribed minimum varies with the area of the principal display panel. The permitted unit lists come from ordinary retail practice rather than from the Second Schedule. The legal sources page lists every one of them and what must be done to each.

Using an unconfirmed interpretation to support enforcement action would be improper. The software makes that hard by printing the flag, and impossible to do unknowingly, but it cannot prevent it.

If you report a product

Report what you actually saw. A report you know to be false wastes the time of people whose job is to protect consumers, and the submission is logged with the network address it came from.

Attach photographs you took or are entitled to share. Do not attach anyone else's private information that has nothing to do with the product.

Keep the reference you are given. It is the only way to check progress, and it works together with the contact detail you supplied. Neither alone is enough, which is deliberate.

Submitting a report starts no legal proceeding and creates no entitlement to a particular outcome, to a timescale, or to be told what an officer decided. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and any other law are unaffected by anything on this page, and this system is not a substitute for them.

If you hold an officer account

Act inside your role and your jurisdiction. The software enforces both, and a request for a record outside your jurisdiction returns 404 rather than 403 so that its existence is not confirmed. Do not attempt to work around that.

Record decisions you can defend. Every finding you confirm, correct or reject is written against your name in an append-only trail that cannot be edited afterwards, including by an administrator, because the database refuses the operation.

Do not approve a rule version you wrote. The software refuses it and returns self_approval_refused. Do not approve one that has not been simulated against every mandatory scenario; that is refused too. These are separations of duty, not obstacles.

Your account is yours. Do not share credentials. Sessions are revocable and every sign-in attempt is recorded with its address.

Content you submit

You keep whatever rights you already had in a photograph you upload. Submitting it grants the operator of the deployment permission to store it, process it and reproduce it inside inspection records and reports, for the purpose of handling your report and for no other purpose.

An image that becomes evidence in an inspection is retained as part of that record. It cannot be withdrawn once a report relying on it has been issued, for the same reason an exhibit cannot be taken back out of a file.

Prohibited use

  • Submitting reports you know to be false, or submitting in volume to obstruct.
  • Uploading malware, or content you have no right to share.
  • Attempting to reach records outside your authorisation, or to escalate your own role.
  • Automated scraping, or load intended to degrade the service for others.
  • Presenting any output of this system as a government determination, or as carrying statutory force.
  • Using an interpretation whose legal authority flag is false to support enforcement action.

Availability

No availability is promised. This deployment runs without redundancy, without monitoring and without a support rota. It may stop at any time and data may be lost if it does, because no backup schedule runs.

What a real deployment needs before it can promise anything is documented in the repository, under docs/DEPLOYMENT.md. None of it is satisfied here.

Liability

The software is provided as it stands, with no warranty of any kind. It has not completed the independent security, accessibility, legal or user-acceptance testing that a production system needs, and the security page lists what is absent.

Because no organisation operates this deployment, no organisation assumes liability for it. Anyone who deploys it takes on responsibility for what it then does, including for the correctness of any rule interpretation they approve and for any decision an officer records through it.

Suspension of access

An administrator can disable any account at any time. An account is locked automatically for 15 minutes after repeated failed sign-ins, and sign-in attempts are limited to 20 per network address in any five-minute window.

Disabling an account does not remove the records it created. Decisions stay attributed to the person who made them, which is the point of attributing them.

Governing law

Conduct on this system is subject to the law of India, including the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

No forum for disputes is named, because there is no operator to bring a dispute against. A deploying authority must state its own forum here.
