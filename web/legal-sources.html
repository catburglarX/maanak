Why nothing is verified

The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 came into force on 1 April 2011 under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009. That much is confirmed.

The rule text itself could not be retrieved from an authoritative source while this software was built. Searching for the declaration text returned commentary and state-level guidance rather than the rule. Searching for the character-height table returned unrelated foreign regulations. A state Legal Metrology guideline document was published as a PDF that could not be read programmatically.

At that point there were two options. Transcribe citations from memory and present them as verified, or record them as supplied text and flag every one as unconfirmed. The second is what happened. A confident citation that turns out to be wrong is worse than an honest gap, because the mistake surfaces in front of somebody contesting a finding.

What is verified

The mechanics are implemented and tested, and that is a separate question from whether a citation string is correct. What the engine does holds regardless:

  • A declaration absent from fully captured evidence is treated as non-compliant.
  • A declaration that could not be read is never treated as a violation.
  • Unit price arithmetic is exact and rounds half-up to the paise.
  • Quantity band boundaries do not overlap: lower inclusive, upper exclusive.
  • A rule not in force on the inspection date does not apply, and neither does one that had already ended.
  • A recorded exception removes the requirement.
  • Character height is never inferred from pixels.
  • Only approved or active versions can affect an inspection.
  • The author of a version cannot approve it.
  • Approval is refused until the simulator covers every mandatory scenario.

One item on this page is implemented from a published specification and confirmed arithmetically: the GS1 modulo-10 check digit. The calculation is worked through by hand in the test suite against the identifier 890103086527 and the result asserted. It is the only citation-like claim here that has been checked end to end.

The character height rule

A minimum height in millimetres cannot be derived from a photograph. Pixels become millimetres only when the scale is known, and the same pixel height means different millimetres at different camera distances.

The check refuses to guess. Without an officer measurement it returns additional evidence required and says what is needed. With a measurement, it compares against the threshold together with the stated uncertainty, and a measurement whose band straddles the threshold returns unable to determine.

The consequence matters: an unconfirmed threshold cannot quietly produce a violation.

How to confirm a version

  1. Retrieve the gazette notification in force on the relevant date.
  2. Compare its citation text, requirement and effective window against the stored citation, explanation and effective dates.
  3. Correct anything that differs. A draft version is editable; an approved one is not, and a corrected interpretation becomes a new version so the old one stays inspectable.
  4. Run the simulator and confirm every mandatory scenario passes.
  5. Have a second rule administrator approve it. The author cannot.
  6. Record the statutory confirmation separately, naming who confirmed it and on what basis.

Steps 4 and 5 are enforced by the software. Steps 1 to 3 and step 6 are human work that software cannot do, and this system is explicit about not having done them.

Officers can read each interpretation's citation, explanation and uncertainty note in the workspace under Rules after signing in.

The law in force on the inspection date governs, not the law today. That is why every interpretation carries an effective window and why the engine selects by date rather than using the newest version available.

What Maanak does not decide

  • No penalty, fine, compounding or prosecution.
  • No assertion that a package is genuine. A readable barcode shows an identifier is printed on a package and nothing more.
  • No verification of net quantity. That requires weighing or measuring, and every report states it as a limit.
  • No medical, nutritional or legal advice.
