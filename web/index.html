What has not been verified

The rule interpretations loaded in this workspace are drafts with unverified citations. None has been endorsed by a statutory authority, and each one must be confirmed against the gazette notification by a qualified authority before it is used in enforcement. Where a finding cites an unconfirmed interpretation, the workspace and the printed report both say so.

Maanak does not verify the contents of a package. Net quantity requires weighing or measuring, and every report states that in its limits. A readable barcode shows that an identifier is printed on a package; it is not evidence that the package is genuine.
