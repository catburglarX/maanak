Contact

This deployment has no staffed contact channel. If you need an answer from somebody with authority, section 3 lists the official routes, which are real, free, and unconnected to this software.

Nobody is monitoring this deployment. No organisation operates it, so there is no address, no telephone number and no office hours. A report submitted here reaches a database, not a person on duty. Maanak is not a government service and is not affiliated with any authority.

What you can do here

Report a product
Describe a problem with a packaged product and attach a photograph. You will be given a reference. Contact details are optional, and the form explains what you give up by leaving them out.
Check your report
Needs your reference together with the email address or telephone number you supplied. Both are required, so a reference on its own reveals nothing about you.
Verify a report
If somebody has quoted a report reference at you, this confirms whether it was really issued and whether its content has changed since. It does not disclose the case.

If you are an officer

Sign in through the officer sign-in page. Accounts are created by an administrator. There is no public registration and no self-service password reset that bypasses an administrator.

If you have lost access, the route is whoever administers your workspace. This site cannot help you.

Official consumer channels

For a complaint that needs an authoritative answer, use the Government of India's own machinery. These are independent of this software and cost nothing.

National Consumer Helpline

The Department of Consumer Affairs runs the National Consumer Helpline as a single point of access at the pre-litigation stage. Grievances can be registered in 17 languages.

Formal filing before a consumer commission

Where a complaint needs adjudication rather than mediation, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 provides for filing before the district, state or national commission according to value. Filings are accepted electronically through the E-Daakhil portal at edaakhil.nic.in.

Weights, measures and package declarations

Enforcement of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011 is carried out by the Legal Metrology department of each state or union territory. A complaint about a short-measure package or a missing declaration belongs with the Controller of Legal Metrology for the state where the package was sold. The Department of Consumer Affairs publishes the department list at consumeraffairs.gov.in.

These addresses are not reproduced here, because a stale contact detail on a page like this sends somebody to the wrong office.

Reporting a problem with the software

There is no channel for a security report, an accessibility barrier or a bug, and pretending otherwise would waste your time. The security page sets out what a real deployment has to publish, and the accessibility statement does the same for barriers.

What a deployment must publish

Before this software is used by anybody real, whoever runs it has to replace this page with:

  • The name and postal address of the operating body.
  • A telephone number and an email address, with the hours they are answered.
  • A named officer for questions about personal data, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires, and a grievance route with a stated response time.
  • A separate address for security reports, with a safe-harbour statement.
  • An address for accessibility barriers, and how to request an alternative format.
  • The escalation route when a response does not arrive.
